💰 Bill Payment

User Story Development Guide · CardDemo Module

📋 Overview

Purpose: Allow cardholders to make payments against their outstanding account balance, immediately reducing ACCT-CURR-BAL.

Business Context: Bill payment is a direct write to the account VSAM file. The payment reduces the current balance and increases available credit in real time.

CB00 · COBIL00C

🎯 User Story Templates

  • As a regular user, I want to view my current balance before making a payment so that I know how much to pay.
  • As a regular user, I want to enter a payment amount and submit it so that my balance is reduced immediately.
  • As the system, I want to validate that payment amount does not exceed the current balance so that the account is not over-paid beyond zero.

Acceptance Criteria

Given: account with ACCT-CURR-BAL = $500.00
When:  user enters payment of $200.00 and submits
Then:  ACCT-CURR-BAL updated to $300.00 in VSAM ACCTDATA.PS
And:   confirmation screen shows new balance

Given: user enters payment amount greater than current balance
When:  COBIL00C validates the amount
Then:  error message "Payment exceeds balance" displayed
And:   no update made to VSAM

🔧 Technical Foundation

ComponentResponsibility

Dependencies

  • sign-on: ACCT-ID from COMMAREA identifies which account to pay

📊 Complexity Guidelines

Simple (1–2 pts)Display current balance on payment screen
Medium (3–5 pts)Process payment with validation; update balance atomically
Complex (5–8 pts)Partial payment scheduling; EFT integration; payment history tracking
